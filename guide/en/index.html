SWINGLOG / IPHONE + APPLE WATCH

SwingLog Full User Guide

Practice with Apple Watch, manage distances and scores during a round, and review your records on iPhone. This guide covers the implementation in version 1.0 · build 23, reviewed on October 18, 2026. Values in examples are illustrative. Screens vary with device, language and permissions.

Getting started

  1. Install SwingLog on your paired iPhone and Apple Watch, then open both apps. iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later are required.
  2. Read the iPhone introduction. Driver and 7-iron distances on the last page are optional. You can enter or change them later in Settings → Manage Clubs.
  3. Check language, metres/yards and handedness in Settings. Wrist and Digital Crown orientation follow the Watch system settings by default. Check your actual wearing arrangement before using the app's overrides.
  4. Open Practice Mode on Watch and complete the three-swing calibration when prompted. Prepare a course before starting Round Mode.

A calibrated Watch with the required settings and course can operate without constant interaction with iPhone. Transfers may wait for connectivity. The online guide, course downloads, purchase verification and iCloud sync may need internet access.

On Watch, choose Practice Mode or Round Mode. Practice has Swing, Tempo and End Activity pages. A round has the main page in the centre, Hazards to its left and Score to its right. Swipe horizontally between pages; use the Digital Crown to scroll long content.

The Watch indicator in the iPhone header reports immediate communication availability. Waiting is not necessarily an error: the Watch app may be closed or temporarily unreachable. A connected indicator does not certify that every pending record has arrived.

Practice: calibration and counting

  1. Start Practice Mode on Watch.
  2. If calibration is required, start it and make three swings representative of your practice swing size. You can cancel calibration.
  3. Select the club on the Swing page and tap Start. Accepted swings update the activity counter and today's count.
  4. Check today's total/goal and progress bar. Change your target in iPhone → Swings → Change Goal; the change is sent to Watch.
  5. Stop pauses detection; Start resumes it. Stop detection before recalibrating.
  6. Use the End Activity page to finish and review the summary. Stopping tempo practice alone does not end the whole activity.

Detection uses wrist motion; it does not reliably count putts or every small chip. Detected swings are not a ball-contact count or your score. For missed or excessive detections, check wearing orientation and recalibration first, then sensitivity in Settings. The current range is 70–130%: lower is more sensitive; higher is more conservative.

Pro's Settings → Swing Intensity Sounds gives audio feedback based on movement intensity relative to calibration. It does not measure clubhead speed or actual shot distance.

Select a date in the iPhone Swings calendar to review that day; trends compare counts with the goal over time. Settings controls language and metres/yards. Changing distance units changes presentation, not the physical distance stored.

Practice: tempo and haptics

On iPhone's Tempo tab, configure backswing and downswing durations, repetition gap and haptic theme. Select a category on the Watch Tempo page and start the repeating rhythm. Stop before changing categories. Pro's iPhone sound practice works independently of Watch; the Watch haptic preview is a separate action.

Iron tempo is available without Pro. Pro adds driver, wedge and putter categories, skill presets and other practice options. Putter rhythm guidance does not mean putts are automatically detected or recorded. Editing durations away from a preset can remove its selected highlight.

Measured tempo is separate from your configured practice target. It is backswing duration ÷ downswing duration: 0.90 s and 0.30 s give 3.0:1. Approximately 3:1 is a common full-swing reference, not a universal quality grade.

Motion recording and impact sound

Under Swings → Swing Motion Analysis, Pro offers these controls:

  • Impact Sound Detection: independent of motion-log recording. It can refine the impact time used for Watch tempo, but cannot certify every ball strike.
  • Swing Motion Recording: retains detailed practice sensor data. Existing motion logs remain accessible when recording is off.
  • High-Frequency Sampling: appears in advanced options when motion recording is enabled. Supported Watches can collect additional detail; capability and actual rate vary. Basic detection remains at 50 Hz. More data can mean larger files and slower transfer.

Turning recording off and on preserves the high-frequency preference and independent sound preference. Recording, speedometer and waveform indicators beside the Watch heading describe settings. The sound-assisted / motion-based icon beside measured tempo describes the evidence actually used for that swing. An enabled waveform indicator does not mean a sound match was used every time.

Round Mode automatically records motion independently of the practice logging switch, always at 50 Hz. It enables impact-sound timing for the round. Battery Saving Mode limits microphone use to relevant windows, so some swings may have no sound evidence.

Stars and your own reference swings

Pro users can tap the star for the latest swing on Watch. An outline is unmarked; a filled star is marked. A marked practice swing is retained and transferred even if practice motion recording is off. Mark it before the next swing replaces the latest target.

Stars also appear in the iPhone list. Change the star or club in a log's summary. Personal Reference Comparison uses other accepted, starred swings with the same club; the current swing is excluded. Each metric requires at least five usable reference values. Collecting roughly 10–20 satisfying swings helps you assess how stable your reference set is.

Timing and tempo compare with the reference median and spread. Forearm-direction comparison uses relative Watch orientation from the beginning of the recording, with matching handedness, wrist and crown metadata. Insufficient or widely scattered references produce unavailable or unstable results. This measures similarity to your own swings, not an ideal posture, clubface angle, cupping or scooping.

Before a round: clubs and courses

  1. In Settings → Manage Clubs, show the clubs you use and check their names and reference distances. Tap a club to edit it.
  2. Enter driver and 7-iron distances to estimate other default clubs. Both must be positive, and driver must be at least 10 m longer. Estimates are starting points. Manually edited distances for other clubs survive later re-estimation. Putter and Other are not estimated this way.
  3. Club changes are collected into a complete-list transfer. Check its status or request sync at the bottom. Default clubs can be hidden; Pro also supports custom clubs and reordering.
  4. In Settings → Round Mode → Manage Courses, select one course, then use Send to Apple Watch. Confirm its name on Watch before playing. The Watch uses the one transferred course.

The in-app + creates a Lite course with reference coordinates. For richer courses with green boundaries, tee areas, hazards and par, download, create or share data at SwingLog Course. Use a desktop browser for editing. Import the downloaded JSON through the course import action in Round Mode settings.

Hole number and green reference coordinate are the basic data. Boundaries, hazards, tee areas and par are optional. Missing information may not appear. The large green distance is to the saved reference point, which may not be the day's actual pin position.

Starting a round and reading the main page

Round Mode requires Pro. Choose it on Watch, check the course, select any starting hole from 1–18 and tap Start Round. Shotgun starts are supported. You can resume an unfinished round when available.

The main page shows hole, optional par, large green-reference distance, front/back distances and the recommended or manually selected club. Tap the hole to change it directly. Club recommendations use remaining distance and your reference distances; they do not fully account for wind, slope, lie or hazards.

Starting a personal-shot measurement restores automatic recommendations for the next shot. Driver is excluded from automatic suggestions while a personal shot is being measured or after a shot distance is saved on that hole. You may still select Driver manually. A manual next-club selection takes priority. The club attached to an active measurement and the club selected for the next shot can differ.

GPS and distance readiness

Tap the GPS icon at the main page's upper left for details. Green means ready; orange indicates acquisition, accuracy improvement or a need for a fresh position; red indicates denied or unavailable location. The icon is in the fixed toolbar near the system clock, so it stays in place while the page scrolls.

Green-distance display and precise shot measurement have different requirements. A distance may be visible while measurement buttons are disabled. Shot measurements generally require a location within five seconds and with horizontal accuracy within 10 m. The display may retain the last valid green distance during a brief loss, so a visible number is not proof of a fresh location.

Wait in an open area. If the swing's original position was not captured, a later position after walking is not silently substituted. Force-quitting and relaunching does not restore an active measurement's GPS origin.

Measuring your shot

  1. Confirm the club before swinging. The Watch needs the detected swing and a location matched to its time.
  2. Tap Measure Shot Distance. Even if you have walked, the origin is the swing location, not where you tapped. There is no separate elapsed-time limit while it remains the latest eligible swing in the current hole with no saved distance.
  3. At your ball, tap Save Distance. This saves horizontal point-to-point distance. It can include roll, not just carry. It is neither a flight trajectory nor the length of your walking route.
  4. Use Cancel if started incorrectly. A previously saved swing cannot be started again as a duplicate measurement.

During measurement, the shot's club · distance remains visible. Recommendations above concern the next shot and do not rewrite the active measurement's origin or club. The star marks a good swing; it does not save a distance.

Correcting the previous shot's club

If you hit 7I while 3W was selected, tap the main club button and choose Correct Previous Shot. Check the target's time and club, then select 7I. Choose Next Shot Club is a separate action affecting the next swing only.

Watch correction updates that swing's round link, motion log and active or saved distance club together. It preserves GPS origin, times, distance and sensor samples. A new swing while the picker is open can invalidate the correction target; reopen it to avoid changing the wrong shot. Individual club edits in iPhone motion-log and distance details should not be confused with this coordinated Watch correction.

Automatic measurement and the next shot

Enable Settings → Round Mode → Automatic Shot Distance on iPhone or the matching toggle at the bottom of the Watch Score page. The default is off and both sides sync. New eligible swings with accurate timestamp-matched GPS start provisional measurements without a confirmation modal. Turning it off preserves a measurement already in progress.

If you swing again before ending the previous measurement, one of these happens. The next-shot completion logic also applies to a measurement you started manually while automatic starts are off.

EvidenceResult and action
Time, movement, GPS and strong matching sound all qualifyThe previous distance saves at the next swing location. Undo is available for 20 seconds. With automatic measurement on, the next measurement starts there.
Time, movement and GPS qualify, but sound is weak or absentDistance freezes at a possible endpoint with Save / Continue. Save uses that captured position; Continue resumes from the original origin.
Movement is short or GPS is insufficientThe existing measurement continues. Save or cancel manually at the intended destination.

Automatic completion requires at least 30 seconds between swings, movement greater than 25 m plus checks accounting for GPS error, and strict sound criteria. Short approaches and poor acoustic conditions may not qualify. If further swings occur after a candidate, check the proposed next target's club and time. Other actions such as measurement changes or hole changes may close the undo window.

Practice swings during a round

Closely spaced round swings are kept as provisional candidates at a shot location. A repeat within 20 seconds, without positive movement evidence, can replace the preceding unprotected candidate. Subsequent GPS evidence that you have left the location helps retain the last candidate.

Stars, explicit measurement choices and club corrections can protect a swing. Longer intervals or uncertain evidence may retain more records. This is not perfect practice-versus-ball-contact recognition. Motion-log retention and the independent detection counter can differ. Always check your score yourself.

Hazards and free measurement

Swipe right from the main page to reach the left-hand Hazards page. Distance ranges are shown for available bunker/hazard geometry. Obstacles missing from the course data cannot be shown.

At the bottom, Free Distance Measurement → Start Distance Measurement measures any two locations, including a companion's shot. Start at the origin, tap End Measurement at the destination, then read the frozen result. Close it or measure again when finished. Cancel discards an active free measurement.

Free measurement creates no shot record, club link or round entry. It can run alongside your personal-shot measurement; ending one does not stop the other. Both use the wearer's current position as the endpoint, so finish each at its own intended destination.

Scoring, changing holes, breaks and finishing

Swipe left from the main page to the right-hand Score page. Strokes means that hole's total including putts. Putts are optional and cannot exceed total strokes. There is no separate penalty field: include penalties in your final stroke total. A companion's separate scorecard is not supported.

Changing a value saves it. Initial values are the hole's par (or 4 if unknown) and up to 2 putts, not detected swings plus two. Merely opening the page does not confirm these defaults. Next Hole: N confirms the displayed score and advances. Shotgun order wraps from 18 to 1 and stops before repeating the starting hole.

With a personal measurement running, Next Hole offers Save Distance & Next Hole / Cancel Measurement & Next Hole / Back. Saving may wait for adequate GPS. With tee geometry and the previous score saved, arrival at the next tee can suggest a change, but GPS never forces a hole change.

For a break, choose Round → Pause. Motion, location, microphone and other activity sensors stop until Resume. Finish or cancel both personal and free measurements before pausing. The round's start-to-end duration includes the break.

End opens Save and End / End Without Saving / Continue Round. Save records the finish time and sends the round and scores to iPhone. Don't Save discards only the round and scores; retained swings and saved distances remain. Continue returns to the activity. Confirming the end cancels unfinished measurements, so save any needed distance first.

Battery saving

The iPhone round setting and Watch Score-page Battery Saving Mode control the same preference. Every round uses 50 Hz and batches record transfers. Saving mode additionally limits microphone listening to relevant movement/stillness windows and relaxes GPS demand while travelling without an active measurement. Active personal and free measurements keep high GPS accuracy demand.

Detection continues, but sound evidence may be missed while the microphone is off or restarting. Motion-based tempo and manual distance saving remain useful. Pause for breaks. Runtime varies with Watch model, battery health, round duration and GPS environment; completing 18 holes is not guaranteed.

Motion-log list and details

Open Swings → View Motion Logs on iPhone. Logs are grouped by date and show recording-start time including seconds, club and star. Combine period (all/today/7 days/30 days), club, star and recognition filters. Edit preserves the filtered scope; select all or a day group to delete selected logs. Swipe left to delete a single record.

Details show linked measured distance when available, time, club/star, peak acceleration and rotation rate, sample count, tempo and personal comparison. Duration is the capture window, not necessarily backswing plus downswing and not a quality target. It can include movement before and after the swing.

Takeaway, top and impact are estimated from sensor evidence. Backswing is top minus takeaway; downswing is impact minus top. Missing phases may prevent a result. Next Practice Step is deterministic guidance based on capture quality and assessment, not a generative-AI response.

Charts and raw export

Combine Charts overlays acceleration, rotation rate and wrist rotation angle after scaling each to its own maximum at 100%. Compare timing and shape; the different physical units are not equivalent. Turn it off for separate acceleration → rotation rate → wrist angle charts. Gravity remains separate.

  • Acceleration (g): wrist/Watch acceleration magnitude, not ball acceleration.
  • Rotation rate (rad/s): Watch angular-speed magnitude, not clubhead or ball speed.
  • Wrist rotation angle (°): relative Watch orientation, not an isolated wrist-joint bend or clubface angle.
  • Gravity X/Y/Z: gravity components in Watch coordinates, useful for observing orientation changes.

The time axis is elapsed time within the recording. Combined charts focus on meaningful motion; separate charts span the recorded interval. Read estimated phase lines and any sound-candidate marker together, distinguishing a detected sound candidate from the impact source actually used for tempo.

Export Raw Data (JSON) shares all retained samples and metadata. It includes OS-derived orientation, gravity and quaternion values, as well as app-derived magnitudes/timing; “raw” does not mean unprocessed electrical sensor output. It contains no audio recording. High-rate records may be larger and slower to transfer.

Requesting AI analysis

SwingLog builds analysis text locally; it does not generate an AI answer or automatically send data to a service. Use the system share sheet or Copy, then choose an external AI. That service's data policy applies after sharing.

  1. One swing: the detail action includes summary metrics and a series reduced to at most 150 points, prioritising key events and peaks. It is different from the complete JSON export.
  2. Patterns: in the list, choose AI analysis and select 3–10 accepted swings; at least five are recommended. Similar conditions and the same club help comparison. All selected summaries plus representative/differing traces are included.
  3. A round: preview and share the round analysis prompt from its scorecard. It uses only that round's linked swings/distances and hole scores/optional putts, not unrelated practice on the same date.

Interpret numerical observations alongside evidence quality and missing shots. If an AI asserts a ball flight, clubface angle or body posture, check whether Watch data can actually support that claim.

Round and distance history on iPhone

Round / Distance → Round History shows the total and monthly groups. Its All/year filter persists across app launches. A scorecard contains start/end times to the minute, elapsed time including breaks, an OUT/IN table and per-hole swings with distance, stars and motion-log links. Missing scores are not zero strokes.

Delete rounds by swiping or using Edit and Select All. Deleting a round does not also delete linked swing or distance records; manage those in their own lists.

The distance tab shows the most recent 25 records and an entry to all records. The 30-day/90-day/all statistics window applies to club summaries; the recent list independently shows the latest 25. Full history supports period, custom dates, club, statistics inclusion and sorting, grouped by date. Load more rows as needed. Edit's Select All includes every filtered match, not just currently loaded rows.

Club summaries use included records for average, median, longest distance and count. A start/end accuracy over 10 m, distance below 20 m or distance above 500 m causes automatic exclusion. Detail can exclude/reinclude otherwise valid records, but cannot force a system-invalid record into statistics. Exclusion does not delete a record.

Measuring with iPhone alone

In Round / Distance, choose a club in the quick measurement section and tap Start. Stay at the origin while GPS is acquired. End at the destination, review the result, then Save or measure again. Measurement and result viewing are free; saving a record requires Pro.

This uses iPhone GPS independently of Watch shot/free measurements. Switching tabs preserves a running session; force-quitting does not promise recovery. Its recording time is when Start was pressed, separate from save time. Watch distances linked to swings use the swing capture's start time.

Sync, backups and deletion

Watch records are retained locally and then transferred to iPhone. Battery batching and provisional swing handling can delay visibility. The round journal/scorecard reaches iPhone after ending and saving the round. Pending transfers retry when connectivity returns.

Settings → iCloud Backup shows account/sync status and the last successful sync. The phone store syncs to private iCloud, including all motion logs. Deletions also sync: this is different from a separate historical backup file. Purchase access is checked through the App Store, not through iCloud records.

Pro's JSON Export Data / Import Data stores and restores records and supported settings. Import merges by identifiers and update times; course-only export is separate. Device-local options such as high sampling, sound, automatic measurement and battery saving, permissions and subscriptions are not all restored by a file backup. Recheck settings after restoration. Exported files are yours to manage.

Delete All Data resets the phone data and requests Watch deletion. Allow connectivity/iCloud propagation and check both devices. It does not remove separately exported files or cancel a subscription. Deleting a custom club can also delete its distance records; read the count in the confirmation dialog.

Pro subscription

Core features include today's count/goal, iron tempo and iPhone distance measurement/result viewing. Pro adds Round Mode, motion logs/measured tempo/star comparisons, calendar/trends, distance records/statistics, additional tempo tools, custom clubs/reordering and file backup. Look for each screen's lock indicators.

In Settings → Upgrade to Pro, check the annual price and terms. Eligible accounts see a one-week free trial, followed by automatic renewal at the displayed annual price. Price, currency and trial eligibility come from the App Store; this guide does not promise a fixed price.

Already subscribed? Use Restore Purchases with the same purchasing account, then reopen both apps. Manage Subscription opens Apple's subscription controls. Deleting the app or its data does not cancel the subscription. Apple advises cancelling an unwanted trial renewal at least 24 hours before the trial ends; see Apple's subscription guide.

Xcode's development unlock is not a public subscription. TestFlight purchasing tests and real App Store billing are different environments. If access is delayed on another device or offline, reconnect and check restoration and relaunch.
